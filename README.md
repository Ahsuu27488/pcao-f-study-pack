# PCAO-F Study Pack

A single-page study pack for the **PCAO-F (Panaversity Certified Associate — Foundations)** final exam.

Open `index.html`, or use the published page. Everything is in one file — no build step, no dependencies, no server.

## What is inside

- **Seven domains** with the published weighting, and the three that carry half the marks called out.
- **Scenario method** — the anatomy of an exam scenario, the delegation loop, the diagnostic ladder, and the action-verb boundaries.
- **Ten official crash courses** from the Panaversity study list, each with the domains it feeds and a time estimate.
- **26 flashcards** for a fast recall pass.
- **180 exam-style questions** in three 60-question sets, each set balanced across the seven domains and across all four answer positions.
- **A timed mock** — 120 minutes, question palette, scaled score out of 1000, pass at 720, per-domain breakdown, and a full answer review.

## Languages

The interface, the study notes and every answer explanation are available in **English**, **Roman Urdu** and **Urdu** — switch in the top-left. Urdu flips the whole page right-to-left.

Question stems and options stay in **English**, because that is the language of the real exam.

## Notes

The time estimates on the study plan and on each course are planning figures, not official durations — adjust them to your own pace after the first course.

Exam logistics follow the most recent official notice circulated in the GIAIC group. Always confirm your slot, date and venue with the group before the day.
